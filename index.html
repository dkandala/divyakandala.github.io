Art, Technology, and Human Experience

Growing up, I was always drawn to creating through different mediums: drawing, painting, and hands-on projects. My love for design became a defining part of who I am.

That interest grew into a passion for the intersection of creativity and technology. Through Information Science, I found joy in blending aesthetics with functionality through coding, front-end development, and user experience design.

I aspire to design intuitive, accessible, and impactful digital experiences that enhance how we interact with technology.
