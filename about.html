Who is Divya?

Artist · Designer · Developer

Art, Technology, and Human Experience

Growing up, I was always drawn to creating through various mediums: drawing, painting, or hands-on projects. I took art classes outside of school and consistently chose art over other electives. My love for design became a defining part of who I am.

While art was my starting point, I became increasingly intrigued by the intersection of creativity and technology. This led me to explore Information Science, where I discovered my passion for coding, front-end development, and user experience design. From crafting meeting posts as Marketing Chair of the Telugu Student Association to prototyping apps in Figma during my interaction design class, I found joy in blending aesthetics with functionality.

Over time, I’ve expanded my skills to include Python programming, data ecosystems, and user research. I aspire to design intuitive, accessible, and impactful digital experiences that enhance how we interact with technology. I’m looking to pursue a User Experience Designer or Research role where I can continue to grow professionally.

Take a look at my portfolio to see how I bring ideas to life at the intersection of art and technology.
